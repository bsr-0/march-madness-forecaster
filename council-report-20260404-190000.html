Core Dispute

Is the signal real (Expansionist) or pure artifact (Contrarian, Outsider)? Testable: rerun at equal sample sizes.

Where the Council Clashes

The Expansionist claims the signal is real and will improve after fixing parity. Three advisors treat it as entirely artifact-driven. This is a testable hypothesis — the rerun will resolve it. The Expansionist's enthusiasm for Massey disagreement, betting lines, and grid-search tuning is premature if the corrected baseline is mediocre.

Blind Spots the Council Caught

  • Overfitting: No held-out validation set. The optimizer has seen all 17 years of ESPN data. Reserve the last 3-4 years before any tuning.
  • Pareto variance collapse: Deterministic selection may suppress bracket diversity, capping upside. Equal counts won't fully fix this.
  • Pool size sensitivity: Optimal strategy for 50-person vs 1000-person pools differs fundamentally. Target pool context undefined.
  • 2011 gap: Skipping a year silently changes the denominator. "13/16 years" is actually 13/15 or 14/16.
