2027 is the first uncontaminated prospective evaluation of the complete
bracket-generation system. At the time of this freeze the 2027 tournament has not
been played and no 2027 outcome exists anywhere in this repository.

This document is intentionally contractual. **Do not edit its substance after the
freeze.** If the system changes, create `PROSPECTIVE_2027_v2.md`, state what
changed and why, and record that the v1 prospective claim is void.

## Why 2026 is not the holdout

2026 was considered and rejected. It cannot serve as an out-of-sample benchmark:

- it sits inside `BACKTEST_YEARS` (2011–2026) and is part of every reported backtest
- the production strategy `meta_region_poolaware` was selected on a 15-year window
  containing it
- `CLAUDE.md` records a modelling conclusion drawn directly from its outcome:
  *"2026 upset-year anomaly blocks further gains with current feature set"*
- the strategy-engine research (FINDINGS.md 6f–6i) used 2026 as one of three
  replication seasons

**2026 is permanently classified as an in-sample integration/regression season.**
It is useful for pipeline correctness, provenance validation, candidate-bank
integrity, Python/browser parity and rendering. Its predictive accuracy, bracket
score and P(1st) must never appear as evidence of out-of-sample performance.

## What the freeze covers

The authoritative machine-readable specification is
`configs/frozen/prospective_2027.json`, generated by introspecting the live code
rather than transcribed by hand. `tests/test_frozen_2027_spec.py` fails CI on any
drift.

**Model** — training cutoff season, train-year list, 12-feature vector and its
keys, blend alpha default, noseed architecture.

**Tournament engine** — log5 pairwise construction (with a numeric probe),
scenario-bank noise assumption (`noise_std = 0.0`), bank size, the propagation
function, and the direction rule: *pairwise → simulator → marginals, never
reversed*.

**Candidate selection** — the sampler (champion quotas with a floor, then EV
strata within champion), `min_per_champion`, `ev_strata`, target candidate count,
P(1st) trial count, pool size, opponent model, common-random-numbers requirement,
both objective definitions, the hierarchical diversity algorithm, K, the scoring
system, and the team-identity scoring mode.

**Preferences** — the seven predicates, and the rule that user-facing frequencies
come from the full scenario bank and never from the candidate artifact.

**Product** — the three v1 strategies, what is excluded from v1, and the required
P(1st) disclosure.

## Allowed and not allowed

Freezing the system does **not** mean ignoring 2027 data. Information a user would
genuinely have had before tip-off is exactly what the system consumes.

**Allowed during development**

- any information available before the stated prediction cutoff for that artifact
- historical seasons through 2026, subject to the frozen training specification
- bug fixes that do not alter a frozen parameter (with the CI gate proving it)

**Not allowed**

- 2027 tournament outcomes
- post-cutoff information of any kind
- tuning any frozen parameter in response to a 2027 result
- regenerating the official 2027 brackets after seeing later information and
  presenting the new output as the original prediction

Every generated artifact must carry an explicit prediction cutoff. Data
availability and outcome availability are separate questions and are recorded
separately.

## The three v1 strategies

| Strategy | Objective | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust the Model** | maximise expected bracket points | none |
| **Win My Pool** | maximise P(1st) | none |
| **Your Preference** | user-selected objective | user-selected preference |

The third is deliberately *not* a new mathematical objective. It is one of the two
measured objectives applied inside a user-chosen constraint, which is more honest
than inventing a blend formula that has never been evaluated.

**Excluded from v1**, because they have not been defined or backtested: a balanced
blend objective, a contrarian ownership penalty, and configurable pool size.

**Required disclosure.** P(1st) assumes a 30-opponent pool with ESPN public pick
behaviour. It is not a universal probability of winning any pool, and must never
be presented as one.

## Known gaps, deliberately outside the freeze

Recorded here so they cannot later be mistaken for drift or for oversights.

1. **Training stops at 2025, not 2026.** `TRAIN_YEARS` ends at 2025, so the frozen
   system does not train on the 2026 season even though 2026 concluded before this
   freeze and would legitimately be available to a 2027 prediction. This was left
   unchanged because altering it is a model change. Adding 2026 is a reasonable v2
   decision and **requires a version bump** — it is not a bug fix.

2. **`noseed` and `blend` baselines are stale.** The train/serve skew fix
   (FINDINGS.md 6c) landed after the last full backtest of those bases, and
   `blend_alpha` has not been refitted against the working model. Neither base is
   used by the v1 product.

3. **The frontend does not exist yet.** No UI is frozen. Building it cannot change
   a frozen parameter; if it appears to require one, that is a signal the parameter
   was wrong, not that the freeze should bend.

4. **Play-by-play derived features are outside the frozen pipeline.** The
   clutch/shooting/minutes backfill in progress feeds future modelling work and is
   not consumed by the v1 candidate artifact.

5. **One bounded provenance exception.** `seed_pick_model._HISTORICAL_WIN_RATES` is
   a hardcoded 1985–2025 constant used only by the shared P(1st) referee. For 2027
   it is clean, since all its data precedes the season.

## What April 2027 should be able to show

The purpose of this freeze is that after the 2027 tournament it is possible to
state, without reconstruction or trust:

- exactly what was known, and when
- exactly what was frozen, verified by hash against this commit
- exactly which brackets were recommended, for which strategy and preferences
- exactly what happened

That holds whether the result is good or bad, which is the point. A prospective
experiment that can only be reported when it succeeds is not an experiment.
