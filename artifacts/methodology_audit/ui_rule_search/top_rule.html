1 · The rule

Shot defense → Shooting (eFG%) → Shot defense

Round of 64, Round of 32, Elite Eight, Final Four and the final: the team that allows the lower effective field-goal %. Sweet 16 only: the team that shoots the better effective field-goal %. Ties go to the better seed.

Simplest of 72 survivors, and the simplest of the 14 that use no composite rating, rank, or seed. 5 · What it is not

  • Not a model, not validated, never scored. No P(1st), no EV, no track record. Nothing in production scoring, selection, the candidate artifact, the fitted evaluation or the track record reads it.
  • Found after the fact. A rule that reproduces the last season is a description of that season. 0 of 72 Final-Four-+-Champion survivors repeat in any of the 13 older seasons — the short-history warning, not a survivor count.
  • The specific-over-rating preference is a legibility choice, not a claim. It reorders equally simple, equally fitted survivors; it never changes which seasons a rule reproduces or its generalisation.
  • Experimental at every surface — strategy-table row, headline tag, panel and export header — and always a client-side feature of the site.
