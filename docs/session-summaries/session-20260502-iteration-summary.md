# Iteration Summary: Construction-First → Opponent-Aware (2026-05-01/02)

## Where We Are

**`meta_region_poolaware` achieves 11.9% P(1st)** — 4x the seed baseline (3.1%), validated at p=0.0076 after multiple comparison correction across 20+ strategies on 14 LOYO years.

The system generates ~25 diverse candidate brackets per year (varying construction mode, probability base, risk level, and forced champion), then simulates each against the opponent field to select the one most likely to finish first. The key insight: optimizing P(beat field) instead of E[points] is a fundamentally different axis that broke through the 8% ceiling.

## What We Tried and Killed

- GBM learned model (4.6%) — worse than raw probability-based construction
- GBM probabilities fed into construction modes — less calibrated than torvik, hurt P(1st)
- SA construction — fundamentally broken (1-2%)
- 14 Kaggle/academic techniques — none beat region construction
- Champion pick optimization — irrelevant to P(1st) (proven by experiment)
- Upset specialist classifier — null result (7.9%, can't beat beam search on coin-flip games)
- Upset detector as poolaware candidate (2026-05-03) — calibrated probs + merged 8-feature model + R32 boost added ~18 candidates. A/B: 10.93% WITH vs 11.20% WITHOUT. Selected 1/15 years, hurt P(1st). Extra candidates add noise.
- Volatility-adapted risk signal — regressed GBM, killed

## How meta_region_poolaware Works

**File:** `scripts/mc_pool_backtest.py`, search for `meta_region_poolaware` dispatch block (~line 3070).

**Candidate generation:**
- 5 risk levels: 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9
- 5 probability bases: torvik, massey_avg, massey_best, blend (from base_round_probs), 80/20 torvik-massey blend
- 2 construction modes: `region_top_n`, `exhaustive_champion`
- 4 forced champions (one per 1-seed, region_top_n only)
- Deduped by bracket content → ~19-31 unique candidates per year

**Selection:** For each candidate, simulate 200 tournament outcomes × 30 opponents. Count how often the candidate scores highest. Pick the candidate with max P(1st).

**Key probability bases used:** `torvik_rp`, `base_round_probs["massey_avg"]`, `base_round_probs["massey_best"]`, `base_round_probs["blend"]`. These are built at the top of `_run_one_year()`.

## Plan Forward — Concrete Next Steps

### Priority 1: Improve the opponent model (highest expected impact)

**Current state:** Opponent brackets are generated by `generate_opponent_brackets()` using seed-based probabilities and ESPN national pick distributions. This is generic — it doesn't model THIS pool's specific behavior.

**What exists:** `data/pool_history/pool_hist_results.json` has 105 real brackets from our actual pool (2023: 18, 2024: 25, 2025: 32, 2026: 30). Module `src/simulation/pool_history_opponent_model.py` may have infrastructure.

**What to build:** Train the opponent model on real pool brackets instead of generic ESPN picks. The pool has specific biases (chalk-heavy? regional? contrarian?) that a behavioral model can capture. The poolaware selector would then optimize against a more realistic opponent field, improving selection accuracy.

**How to test:** Replace `generate_opponent_brackets()` in the poolaware dispatch with a pool-history-based generator. LOYO: for test year Y, train opponent model on pool brackets from years ≠ Y. Compare P(1st) vs current 11.9%.

### Priority 2: More candidate diversity (medium impact, low effort)

**What:** The `blend` probability base was selected 7/14 years — it's the single most useful base. Explore:
- More blend ratios (70/30, 60/40 torvik/massey instead of only 80/20 and 90/10)
- Walk-forward Massey best-system selection (the Massey composite includes ~150 systems; using the historically best-performing one per year instead of the average)
- Contrarian probability base adjustments for candidate diversity

**How to test:** Add new entries to the candidate generation loop in the poolaware dispatch. Rerun backtest. Check if the selector picks any of the new candidates.

### Priority 3: Poolaware + upset specialist combination (speculative)

**What:** The upset specialist alone was a null result (7.9%). But upset-adjusted brackets as CANDIDATES for poolaware selection might work — the specialist produces a different bracket that the selector might choose in specific years.

**How:** Add upset-specialist-adjusted region brackets to the candidate pool. The specialist module exists at `src/prediction/upset_specialist.py`.

### What NOT to do

- Don't try to improve game prediction accuracy (BSS=0 ceiling, 14 techniques already tested)
- Don't feed GBM probabilities into construction (proven to hurt)
- Don't build SA-based construction modes (fundamentally broken)
- Don't optimize champion pick in isolation (proven irrelevant to P(1st))
- Don't add features to the GBM meta-selector (#9, #10, #3, #12 all had zero effect)
